Cafe Arian is ready to use.

On the first launch, enter your name. The app opens directly after that, without a password. You can change the name in Settings. Upgrading keeps all existing business data and history.

The installer creates a Start menu shortcut. If you selected the desktop option, it also creates a desktop shortcut. To pin the app to the taskbar, launch it, right-click its taskbar icon, and choose "Pin to taskbar".
